CA Foundation Course Details

Paper 1: Principles and Practice of Accounting

Fundamental principles of accounting, double entry system, and basic accounting concepts.

Preparation of Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account, and Balance Sheet.

Preparation and understanding of bank reconciliation statements.

Paper 2: Business Laws & Business Correspondence and Reporting

Comprehensive study of contract law including formation, performance, and breach of contracts.

Laws governing sale of goods, conditions and warranties, transfer of ownership.

Business communication, letter writing, and report preparation skills.

Paper 3: Business Mathematics and Logical Reasoning & Statistics

Ratio, proportion, partnership, simple and compound interest, time value of money.

Number series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, and analytical reasoning.

Statistical analysis, measures of central tendency, probability, and correlation.

Paper 4: Business Economics & Business and Commercial Knowledge

Theory of demand and supply, production, cost analysis, and market structures.

National income, money and banking, fiscal policy, and international trade.

Business environment, forms of business organization, and current business trends.
